About me

Care that starts with listening.

I became a nursing assistant after caring for my grandmother through her recovery from a hip fracture. Those months taught me that small things, like a warm greeting, an unhurried bath, or noticing a change in appetite, make a real difference to a patient’s day.

Today I work 12-hour shifts on a 24-bed rehabilitation unit, supporting residents with daily living activities, mobility, and post-surgical recovery. I’m known for accurate charting, calm communication with families, and being the teammate who picks up the extra call light.

I’m currently completing prerequisites for an LVN program and looking for a team that values growth, safety, and genuinely person-centered care.
